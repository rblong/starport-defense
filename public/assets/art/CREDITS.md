# Menu art sources

- `battlecruiser-hero.png`: generated ship illustration based on a local classic Terran Battlecruiser silhouette reference; the raw reference stays in the ignored local pack.
- `earth-daymap.png`: NASA Blue Marble, NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio. Blue Marble data: Reto Stockli, NASA/GSFC and NASA Earth Observatory. Official record: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/2915 ; image: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a002900/a002915/bluemarble-2048.png .
- `earth-clouds.jpg`: NASA Blue Marble cloud composite (static source, animated by the game; not live weather). Official record: https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/57747/blue-marble-clouds/77558l . Download mirror: https://phys23p.sl.psu.edu/phys_anim/astro/parallax/cloud_combined_2048.jpg (NASA host TLS was unavailable in this environment).
- Classic terrain files and map reference images are local-only, under ignored `artifacts/starcraft1/` and `public/assets/sc-local/`; see docs/12-terrain-and-recoil-fix.md for sources.
